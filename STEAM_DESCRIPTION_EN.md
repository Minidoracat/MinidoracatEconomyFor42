[hr][/hr]

[h2]✨ What is this?[/h2]
An economy system planned around two currencies, a trading center, an auction house, and conversion of Discord points into in-game currency.

[h2]🚧 Development status[/h2]
Only the MOD project foundation exists at this time. No in-game economy features have been implemented yet.

[h2]🧰 Planned features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Two currencies[/b]: Manage two in-game currencies and player balances
[*] [b]Trading center and auction house[/b]: Let players list, buy, and trade goods
[*] [b]Discord point conversion[/b]: Convert Discord points into in-game currency
[/list]
