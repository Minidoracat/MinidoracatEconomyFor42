description=[h1]💰 Minidoracat Economy for B42[/h1]
description=[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=An economy system for Build 42 [b]dedicated multiplayer servers[/b]: earn Survivor Coins and trade at ATMs and trade stations. [b]No single-player or Host / co-op.[/b]
description=
description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Required UI framework: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library for B42[/url]
description=[*] The server and every player need both mods at the same version; after an update, restart both the server and the game
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Press "[" or click the floating button to open the [b]Economy Center[/b]; away from a terminal you can only browse
description=[*] Walk up to a map ATM or an admin-built terminal and right-click [b]Use economy terminal[/b] to trade
description=[*] Claim your daily online reward on the Rewards page to start earning Survivor Coins
description=[*] Buy and sell in the shop, market or auctions, and collect purchases from your Mailbox
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Two-currency wallet[/b]: Survivor Coin and Cat Coin, with a searchable, filterable statement
description=[*] [b]System shop[/b]: fixed-price purchases, and sell items back to the server when buyback is on
description=[*] [b]Player market and auctions[/b]: sell items from your bag at a fixed price or by auction, at any terminal
description=[*] [b]Player transfers[/b]: send currency straight to another player when the server allows it
description=[*] [b]Mailbox[/b]: purchases, wins and returns arrive here and survive your character's death
description=[*] [b]Rewards and seasons[/b]: daily online rewards, survival milestones, holdings and survival leaderboards
description=[*] [b]Market radio[/b]: tune a radio to a trade station's market broadcast (server option)
description=[*] [b]Paid slots for other mods[/b]: buy or rent extra slots in supporting mods with economy currency
description=[/list]
